NSE7 Enterprise Firewall 7.6

The NSE7 Enterprise Firewall exam tests your ability to design, configure, manage, and troubleshoot enterprise-grade FortiGate deployments. It covers complex multi-device topologies, centralised management via FortiManager, dynamic routing, advanced VPN architectures, and deep security profile logic. Scenario-based reasoning and CLI literacy are critical.
